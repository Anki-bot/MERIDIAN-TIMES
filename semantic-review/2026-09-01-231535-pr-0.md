## P0 — Fidelity checks stop before browser-delivered pixels

**Confirmed:** `scripts/verify-watch-layer-assets.mjs:2000-2068` builds its comparison from canonical source/mask records, while `:2210-2258` checks WebP hash, signature, dimensions, and color metadata. It never decodes and composes the staged compact/expanded WebPs for the required SSIM and per-pixel comparison.

Encoding, alpha, crop, or profile-composition defects can therefore pass when the bytes match their own metadata. The gate must evaluate the exact public bytes at each reference viewport against the `0.995` SSIM and `99.5%`-within-eight thresholds and bind that evidence to the release.
